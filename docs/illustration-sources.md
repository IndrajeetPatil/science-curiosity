# Illustration sources

The book has ten illustrations. An image is credited to an external source
only when the image itself matches that source or has a credit printed on it.
Subject similarity alone is not enough. The per-image record in
[`figures.json`](../figures.json) contains captions, credits, source links,
verification status, and evidence; the PDF includes the reader-facing
acknowledgements.

| Figure | Subject | Current attribution status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dog and mirror | Exact Georgia Pinaud photograph on Wikimedia Commons; public domain. |
| 2 | Buckyballs | Exact NASA/JPL-Caltech artwork PIA13551, cropped. |
| 3 | Antibodies | Exact diagram on Science Aid by Stephen Claydon (2008); educational use with citation. |
| 4 | Electronic circuit | Photographer, original source and licence are unresolved. |
| 5 | Turing test | Exact *Turing Test version 3* by Bilby; public domain. |
| 6 | Brain scans | Creator, original study and licence are unresolved. |
| 7 | Water-screw machine | Exact Böckler engraving on Wikimedia Commons; public domain. |
| 8 | Thermal comparison | Dog and lizard components match NASA/IPAC's Cool Cosmos archive; composite licence is unverified. |
| 9 | Cloaking simulation | Exact creator, source and licence are unresolved; similar simulations were rejected. |
| 10 | Salamanders | Exact photographer, study and licence are unresolved; related images were rejected. |

Credits do not establish a blanket reuse licence for the book or its unresolved
images. Font licences are included in `fonts/`.
