# Illustration sources

The book retains the ten illustrations from its original edition. An image is
credited to an external source only when the retained image itself matches that
source or has a credit printed on it. Subject similarity alone is not enough.
The per-image record in [`figures.json`](../figures.json) contains captions,
credits, source links, verification status and evidence; the PDF includes the
reader-facing acknowledgements.

| Figure | Subject | Current attribution status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dog and mirror | Exact Georgia Pinaud photograph on Wikimedia Commons; public domain. |
| 2 | Buckyballs | Exact NASA/JPL-Caltech artwork PIA13551; cropped in the original book. |
| 3 | Antibodies | Exact diagram on Science Aid by Stephen Claydon (2008); educational use with citation. |
| 4 | Electronic circuit | Photographer, original source and licence are unresolved. |
| 5 | Turing test | Exact *Turing Test version 3* by Bilby; public domain. |
| 6 | Brain scans | Creator, original study and licence are unresolved. |
| 7 | Water-screw machine | Exact Böckler engraving on Wikimedia Commons; public domain. |
| 8 | Thermal comparison | Dog and lizard components match NASA/IPAC's Cool Cosmos archive; composite licence is unverified. |
| 9 | Cloaking simulation | Exact creator, source and licence are unresolved; similar simulations were rejected. |
| 10 | Salamanders | Exact photographer, study and licence are unresolved; related images were rejected. |

Searches covered Wikimedia Commons, NASA image records, the Internet Archive
for Science Aid, institutional pages, and archived Cool Cosmos material.
Search-engine and visual-search access limits are recorded in `figures.json`
rather than replaced with speculative attributions. Credits do not establish
a blanket reuse licence for the book or its unresolved images. Font licences
are included in `fonts/`.
